🎯 Consonant Tug-of-War: How to Play

1. Set Up the Game

Before starting, the teacher chooses the game settings:

  • Category – The teacher can type in a category or choose New to generate a random category.
  • Goal Points – The teacher can use the default number of points or change it to set the winning score.
  • Timer – The teacher can use the default time or change the timer for each turn.

When the settings are ready, the teacher presses Start.

2. Choose a Word

Each team thinks of a word that belongs to the category shown on the screen.

For example, if the category is Animals:

  • Team 1: CAT
  • Team 2: HORSE

The teacher types each team's word into the appropriate Team Word box. When both words have been entered, the teacher presses Play Turn.

3. The Consonants Pull the Board

Each consonant in a team's word pulls its corresponding letter one space toward that team. For example:

  • One N = 1 space
  • Two N's = 2 spaces
  • Three N's = 3 spaces

So, the more times a consonant appears in a word, the further that letter moves. If both teams have the same consonant, both teams pull that letter in opposite directions. The two pulls cancel each other out as appropriate.

4. Score a Point

When a letter is pulled all the way to the end of the board and falls off on a team's side:

  • 🏆 That team scores 1 point.
  • The letter then returns to the middle of the board.
  • It can be pulled again during later turns.

5. Play the Next Turn

The timer controls how long the teams have to think of their words. When the turn is over, the teacher enters the teams' new words and presses Play Turn again. The consonants move according to the letters in the new words. Keep playing until one team reaches the goal score.

🏆 How to Win

The first team to reach the Goal Points set at the beginning of the game wins! For example, if the goal is 5 points, the first team to reach 5 points wins the game.

Think fast. Choose your words carefully. Pull the consonants to your side!
